Snowangel Solitaire privacy policy

Effective September 19, 2026. Publisher: Eden Bound LLC, Topeka, Kansas. Contact: info@glolaw.org.

Snowangel Solitaire does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • Your game progress (levels, wins, snowflake collection, settings) is saved only on your own device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.
  • The app makes no network connections. Its fonts and artwork are built in.
  • The optional "Tip the maker" link opens venmo.com in your web browser. Anything you do there is governed by Venmo's privacy policy, not this one.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
